# Product: SILVERSTONE

## What SILVERSTONE is
A patented engineered stone material fusing stone, copper, and silver using nanotechnology and Vedic material sciences (Vastu Shastra, Ayurveda, Dhatu Shastra). Silver and copper are infused at a material level, not as a coating.

## Product lines
- Wellness Kitchen (flagship).
- Wellness Wardrobe (since 2023).
- Wellness interiors (expanding).
- Porcelain kitchens (sourced from India for the US market).

## The problem SILVERSTONE solves
Modern kitchens are built from compressed wood (MDF, particle board, plywood) bonded with resins. That shift happened in the 20th century for manufacturing profit, not human wellbeing. The category has seen almost no health innovation in roughly 90 years. Wood swells, warps, rots, attracts termites and fungus, burns, and can release formaldehyde from resins. Before industrial manufacturing, the world's kitchens were built in stone.

## The 16 product features: resilience pillar (10)
1. Fire retardant (does not burn).
2. Thermal shock resistant.
3. Impact resistant.
4. Heavy structural load capacity.
5. Stain resistant (turmeric, coffee, oil).
6. Scratch resistant.
7. Waterproof (does not swell in monsoon or water).
8. Extreme weather durable (salt air, humidity, heat).
9. Does not warp.
10. Long-term wear resistant.

## The 16 product features: health and hygiene pillar (6)
1. Termite proof (nothing to eat).
2. Silver and copper infused surface (antimicrobial performance to be cited only with lab reports).
3. Zero formaldehyde (no wood, no resin binders of that type).
4. Fully washable.
5. Fungus and mould resistant.
6. Zero toxins by composition.

## Claims rule
In public content, describe what the material physically does and what it does not contain: does not burn, does not swell, does not stain, contains zero formaldehyde by composition. Health outcome claims require verified lab data and legal review. Never say "cures", "prevents disease", "cancer-safe", "boosts immunity" or "kills 99.9% of viruses". Antimicrobial performance may be cited only with lab reports.

## Ahimsa rule
Ahimsa (non-violence) is a brand value. MAGPPIE is never positioned as a killer of pests or germs. Termites and cockroaches simply find nothing to eat; they are not killed. Say "termites find nothing to eat", never "kills termites".

## Coastal and climate thesis: "Stone Outlasts Everything"
- Coastal kitchens are replaced every 4 to 5 years, against 7 to 10 years inland, because of humidity, salt air, and termites. (Company estimate.)
- Priority cities: Mumbai, Chennai, Kochi, Calicut, Visakhapatnam in India; Los Angeles (wildfire rebuild) and Miami in the USA; Dubai and the Gulf; Bangkok, Singapore, coastal Australia, Japan, UK, Indonesia.

# Experience centre: "Show Not Tell"

## Philosophy
Clients test the claims themselves. No explanation until after the test.

## Proof Lab (10 stations, single-word dares)
BURN IT (fire), SHOCK IT (thermal), DROP IT (impact), LOAD IT (structural), STAIN IT, SCRATCH IT, DROWN IT (water), the termite station, the hygiene station, WEAR IT (long-term simulation).

## Proof Passport, Material Truth Walk and live exhibits
- Proof Passport: clients collect a stamp at each station.
- Material Truth Walk: a demo room comparing damaged MDF beside SILVERSTONE.
- Live exhibits: a termite terrarium and a saltwater submersion chamber.
