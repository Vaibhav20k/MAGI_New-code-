# Website: the wellness movement

## How the website introduces MAGPPIE
The world needs a new kitchen: one that nurtures the people who live in it. Every day, our food, air and homes are filled with unseen threats to our well-being. From the cabinets we cook in to the wardrobes we breathe near, the spaces that should nurture us are working against us. Magppie is not just a brand; it is a wellness movement, bringing deep impact through deep tech.

## The Magppie promise: three commitments, one movement
- Wellness built in stone: the world's first kitchens and wardrobes fully built in patented Silverstone.
- A 25-year guarantee, a reflection of the commitment to long-term satisfaction.
- Complimentary services to maintain the longevity of the investment.

## For the planet
No deforestation. No mining. No compromise. Compressed wood (MDF, particle board, plyboard) leads to deforestation. Natural granite and marble come from mining. Magppie Silverstone needs neither.

# Website: the kitchen

## What a MAGPPIE kitchen is made of
For the first time ever, every internal cabinet, door fascia, countertop, backsplash, accessory and even handle is made in stone: patented Silverstone. No wood. No MDF. No formaldehyde. Every MAGPPIE kitchen is built on the same 15 mm Silverstone wellness carcass, with profile lighting and the 25-year unconditional guarantee.

## Wood kitchen against stone kitchen
- Termites: wood feeds termites, stone starves them. Termites tunnel into wood from inside the panels and feed for years before a single sign shows. Silverstone offers no cellulose, no fibre, no food at all. Termites that arrive at a Magppie kitchen leave hungry.
- Moisture: wood drinks water, stone refuses every drop. Wood pulls water out of the air; in monsoon it swells, and in dark cabinets mould grows unseen. Silverstone is non-porous. Water beads off and leaves.
- Formaldehyde: compressed wood panels can off-gas formaldehyde for years after a kitchen is installed. Silverstone is inert mineral. It cannot release what is not inside it.

## Seven everyday tests ("Why stone makes all the difference")
1. Fire safe: made entirely from stone, the surface does not catch fire or help flames spread. Built for daily Indian open-flame cooking (tadka, roti, deep frying).
2. Water safe: a wooden panel and a Magppie stone sample were placed in water for thirty days. The wood swelled and weakened. The stone stayed exactly the same.
3. Stain safe: non-porous Silverstone does not absorb spills. Coffee, haldi or oil wipes off in a single stroke.
4. Scratch safe: built for daily Indian cooking; regular chopping and knife work do not leave a mark.
5. Impact safe: a heavy ceramic jar was dropped on the surface. The stone stayed intact.
6. More storage: with extra depth and height, Magppie wall cabinets offer up to 62% more storage than standard kitchens, designed to fit large Indian plates, thalis and vessels.
7. High load: the website says each drawer supports up to 80 kg of vessels, groceries and appliances without bending or sagging.
Every claim is physically demonstrated: scratch tests, fire tests, 30-day water immersion, load tests, impact drops.

## Independent test reports named on the website
Certified by Shree Ram Testing Laboratories (ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 9001:2015):
- ASTM E-2180 (bacteria test).
- BS EN 12390 (water and weather proofing: zero permeability under pressure).
- Class 1 across scratch, stain, heat and impact resistance tests. Zero formaldehyde. Termite proof.
- ASTM E-84 (fire): Class A flame spread index.

# Website: wardrobes and vanities

## The Wellness Wardrobe
Clothes sit inside wardrobes for weeks, next to compressed wood. Magppie reimagined the wardrobe in stone: every cabinet, fascia and handle in patented Silverstone. Where wood-based wardrobes swell and warp, a Magppie wardrobe is inert stone from the inside out, termite proof and moisture-stable for decades. 13 accessories: shoe drawer, trouser pull-out rack, low-depth drawer, deep storage basket, jewellery tray, watch winder, card lock, biometric lock (up to 5 fingerprints), built-in safe box, built-in mirror, tie pull-out, pull-out ironing board, suede-lined drawer.

## The Wellness Vanity (launched April 2026)
After kitchens and wardrobes, Silverstone arrives in the bathroom, the most water-intense room of the home, in eight new finishes. Every cabinet, fascia, drawer and handle is Silverstone, replacing wood, MDF and plywood. Non-porous: it does not swell, warp or stain with daily steam and splash. Finishes include Magppie Onyx Gold, Onyx Mystic, Romano, Persian Travertine, Calm, Cloud Stone and Flurry Black.
