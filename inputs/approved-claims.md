## Prohibited / Rejected Claims
- `REJ-001`: Any health outcome claim: "cures", "prevents disease", "cancer-safe", "boosts immunity". (Rejected: needs lab data and legal review.)
- `REJ-002`: "Antibacterial", "antimicrobial", "kills 99.9% of germs or viruses", or any percentage about germs. (Rejected: may be cited only with lab reports, which are not held here.)
- `REJ-003`: "Kills termites", "kills cockroaches", or any wording that shows or praises killing. Ahimsa is a brand value: termites find nothing to eat. (Rejected.)
- `REJ-004`: MAGPPIE kitchens described as steel, stainless steel, wood, MDF, plywood or laminate. They are stone. (Rejected.)
- `REJ-005`: Any number, lifespan, test standard, decibel level, certification or award that is not in this register or in the product knowledge. (Rejected: do not round, extend or extrapolate.)
- `REJ-006`: Naming or attacking a competitor brand. Compare against materials only. (Rejected.)
- `REJ-007`: IPO, valuation, revenue, funding, order book, store targets, prices or discounts. The word "SME". (Rejected.)
- `REJ-008`: The number of complimentary home visits. (Rejected: the number is still being confirmed.)
- `REJ-010`: Any property, colour, finish, thickness or size of the stone that is not in this register or in the product knowledge. Do not name a colour of the stone in the voice-over. (Rejected: describe only what the knowledge states.)
- `REJ-009`: Em dashes or en dashes anywhere in the script. Use commas, periods, colons or parentheses. (Rejected.)
