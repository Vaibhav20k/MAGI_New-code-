# MAGI Crawler Rules

Written for MAGPPIE from the Master Brand Context File (October 2026). The night crawler, the filter and the
knowledge agent all follow this file.

## 1. What we are looking for
- Topics: wellness kitchens, wellness wardrobes and wellness interiors; engineered stone, sintered stone, porcelain and quartz surfaces; stone-built kitchens; healthy and non-toxic building materials; formaldehyde and indoor air quality; termites, monsoon damage, water swelling, stains, fire and wildfire resilience, coastal durability; Vastu and Ayurveda in the home; premium kitchen design, architecture and interiors; climate-resilient homes.
- Content types: blogs and articles, reels and shorts, carousels, campaigns, product and material launches, showroom and experience-centre ideas, test and proof videos.
- Audience we care about: families building or renovating premium homes; architects, interior designers and builders; India first (metros and coastal cities), then the USA, the Gulf and Europe.
- Languages: English first; Hindi is fine.

## 2. Where to look
- Always crawl: the similar brands in seed-sources.md.
- Also OK to discover: premium kitchen and surface brands, material science and healthy-building sites, design and architecture magazines, kitchen and bath industry bodies and shows (NKBA, KBIS, ISFA), architect and designer channels, wellness real estate research.
- Countries and regions: India, USA, Europe, the Gulf.

## 3. What to skip
- Skip topics: politics, religion as argument, caste, adult, violence, celebrity gossip, sports, stock tips.
- Skip pages older than: 18 months, unless they are reference material.
- Skip: login pages, paywalled content, job listings, shopping carts, privacy and cookie pages.

## 4. Crawl limits (industry practice)
- Crawl hours: 8 PM to 8 AM IST.
- Always respect each site's robots.txt and any "noindex" or "nofollow" instruction.
- Wait at least 3 seconds between pages on the same site; never more than 4 sites at once.
- Max clicks away from a good page: 3.
- Read public pages only. Never log in, never pass a paywall, never fill a form.
- Keep the idea and the link, not the text: never store or reuse a full article, caption or script word for word.
- Never collect personal data (phone numbers, emails, home addresses, private accounts).
- One page is read once; unchanged pages are not read again.

## 5. Knowledge approval rules

Every new learning goes into ONE bucket. When unsure, choose HOLD FOR MANAGER.

### AUTO-SAVE (safe), only if seen on 2 or more independent sources
- Content trends: topics, formats, hooks, styles that perform well
- Public engagement numbers (views, likes, comments)
- Keywords and search topics
- Common customer questions, pain points and wishes from public comments
- What similar brands posted (topic, format, date), not their claims
- General industry information that doesn't contradict our knowledge base
- New relevant sites, channels and creators
Every auto-saved item needs: source link, date found, expiry (trends expire after 60 days unless seen again).

### HOLD FOR MANAGER (doubtful)
About MAGPPIE: anything contradicting our knowledge base (for example anything saying MAGPPIE kitchens are steel or wood); claims others make about MAGPPIE; complaints and negative reviews; anything changing our brand voice, positioning or target customer; future plans; anything about the IPO, valuation, revenue or funding.
Numbers and money: prices (ours, other brands', market); discounts, offers, EMI; market size, share, growth stats; sales, revenue, funding.
Other brands: product specs, materials, warranties; price changes, launches, store openings; "us vs them" comparisons; claims of "best", "first", "only", "No. 1".
Health, safety, science: health, wellness, hygiene claims ("non-toxic", "antibacterial", "antimicrobial"); material safety and certifications; lab results; Ayurveda, Vastu, Vedic or ancient-knowledge claims; eco and sustainability claims.
Legal and official: laws, regulations, BIS/ISO standards; awards, rankings; patents; partnerships and tie-ups; lawsuits and disputes.
People: any named person; quotes from real people; celebrity or influencer associations.
Low confidence: found on only one source; forums, anonymous posts, unverified accounts; news under 48 hours old; AI-generated or auto-translated content.

### NEVER SAVE
Personal data (phones, emails, addresses); content behind logins or paywalls; full articles, captions or scripts copied word for word (save the idea + link only); leaked or private documents; politics, religion, caste; adult, violent or hateful content; rumours and gossip; blocked sites; anything unrelated to our industry; anything that praises killing pests (ahimsa is a brand value).

## 6. Daily approval review
- Held items go to a HOLDING AREA and must NOT be used by any agent.
- Every day at 6:00 PM IST, the manager is notified on the dashboard and by a Chrome notification.
- For each item show: what was learned, why it's held (which rule), source links and date, what in the knowledge base it affects.
- Actions: Approve / Reject / Edit and approve / Add note. The manager can also add knowledge by hand, add "never use" rules, and block or trust sites.
- Nothing changes until the manager presses FINALISE REVIEW.
- Rejected items go to a REJECTED LIST and must never be asked again.
- Items not reviewed stay for the next day; auto-reject after 14 days.
