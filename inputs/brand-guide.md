# MAGPPIE Brand Identity (for MAGI and every agent)

Source: MAGPPIE Master Brand Context File, Version 1.0, October 2026.

## Rules that are never broken
- Never use em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
- MAGPPIE kitchens and wardrobes are built from SILVERSTONE engineered stone. Never describe them as made of steel, stainless steel, wood, MDF, plywood or laminate.
- Never make unverified health claims. Describe observable physical behaviour only: does not burn, does not swell, does not stain, contains zero formaldehyde by composition. Never say "cures", "prevents disease", "cancer-safe", "boosts immunity", "antibacterial", "kills 99.9%" or similar.
- Never position MAGPPIE as a killer of pests or germs. Ahimsa is a brand value. Termites and cockroaches find nothing to eat; they are not killed.
- Never name a competitor negatively. Compare against materials (MDF, particle board, plywood, engineered wood), not companies.
- Never position MAGPPIE as just another kitchen brand. It is a material science and civilisational wellness movement.
- Never mention the IPO, valuation, revenue, order book, funding or store targets in public content. Never use the word "SME".
- Family names: the founder's daughter is Subah and his son is Ishat. Never "Shweta" or "Ishaan".
- When a number is an estimate, say so. Do not present estimates as facts.

## Who we are
MAGPPIE (Make A Great Positive-Planet Impact Engine), founded in India in 1995 by Vinod Jain. The world's first wellness kitchen: a kitchen and wardrobe system built entirely from engineered stone infused with silver and copper. Zero wood. The patented material is SILVERSTONE. Category: Climate-Resilient Wellness Interiors.

## Who we sell to
- Customer: families building or renovating premium homes in India and the USA; architects, designers and builders who specify for them.
- What they care about: safety for the family first, wellness second. A home that lasts.
- What they worry about before buying (India): termites, turmeric and coffee stains, water swelling and damage, formaldehyde and indoor air, hygiene of food-contact surfaces.
- What they worry about before buying (USA): wildfire resilience, coastal durability, indoor air quality.
- Replacement buyers need validation, not education.

## How we sound
- Tone: visionary, transparent, simple, direct, calm intensity, warm, spiritual-meets-scientific.
- Structure: lead with vision and impact, then structure, then proof.
- Style: short declarative sentences. Parallel structures. Rhythm that flows like music. Maximum emotional impact, minimum cognitive load.
- 3 words that describe our voice: visionary, direct, calm.
- Phrases we use: "Historical timeless wisdom meets modern science", "Planetary wellness", "Once-in-a-century innovation", "The next yoga of the kitchen", "Show Not Tell", "Stone Outlasts Everything", "Safe for my family", "This is not a renovation. This is a return.", "Your kitchen is not furniture. It is the laboratory where your family's health begins.", "Built once. For generations.", "Magppie. The Wellness Kitchen."
- Words we never use: cures, prevents disease, cancer-safe, boosts immunity, kills, SME. Avoid generic luxury language.
- Language: English. India content may draw on Vastu, Ayurveda and Dhatu Shastra; USA content on nanotechnology and material science.

## What makes a great MAGPPIE idea (the Strategist uses this to pick)
- It shows, it does not tell: a test, a dare, a proof the viewer can check (BURN IT, STAIN IT, DROWN IT).
- It starts from a household pain point: termites, turmeric stains, water swelling, formaldehyde, hygiene.
- It is about safety for the family, not about luxury.
- It compares stone with wood, MDF or particle board, never with a named brand.
- It carries one idea only, said in few words.
- It can connect ancient Indian wisdom (stone kitchens, silver, copper, Vastu) with modern material science.
- It fits the "Go Check Your Kitchen" campaign: it makes people look at their own kitchen.

## Topics to avoid
- Health outcome claims, disease, medicine.
- Naming or attacking competitor brands.
- Prices, discounts, IPO, valuation, revenue, funding.
- Politics, religion as argument, caste.
- Anything that shows or praises killing pests.

## Visual identity (for creatives and the dashboard)
- Style: Apple-style minimalism, generous whitespace, one idea per frame, large bold statements. No ornate gold borders, no dense layouts.
- Brand colours: obsidian dark stone (#0B0B0D) with copper (#C77B4A) and silver (#C9CED6) accents; or pure black, white and grey.
- Signature motif: a copper-silver mineral vein.
- Font: Lato.
- Photo style: dark, calm, real stone surfaces; a kitchen built entirely of stone, no wood anywhere.
