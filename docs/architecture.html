Architecture

AI agent Data and knowledge Outside source or service You and your screen Agent 11 · feedback loop, phase 2 Runs on the Oracle server

The dotted outline is the Oracle server, where MAI and all 11 agents run. The database also lives on that server. Higgsfield (images and video), Apify (social media) and the AI brains are outside services it talks to. Your Mac is only where the system gets built.

Every agent, explained

Think of it as a small marketing team where each member has one job. MAI is the coordinator; the other ten do the work, one after another. Your marketing manager has the final say on anything doubtful.

AGENT 01 · HERMES AGENT

MAI

The coordinator
ALWAYS ON

Listens to you, by voice or text, and decides which agent does what. It starts the crawl at 8 pm, stops it at 8 am, sends the manager's approval request at 6 pm, and remembers how you like things done.

TAKES
Your voice or clicks, the schedule
GIVES
Jobs to every other agent, spoken or written replies to you

You say "Hey MAI, give me new ideas". MAI wakes agents 06 and 07 and reads the 6 ideas back to you.

AGENT 02 · SEARXNG

Discovery

The scout
NIGHT

Finds new websites and channels similar to the ones you gave it. It follows links from good sites and searches for topics from your KB.

TAKES
Your seed sites, crawler rules, KB topics
GIVES
A growing list of new sites worth reading

A kitchen design blog links to an interiors magazine. Discovery adds the magazine to tonight's list.

AGENT 03 · CRAWL4AI + APIS

Crawler

The reader
NIGHT

Visits every site on the list and reads the pages, posts and videos. It turns each one into plain text, without menus, ads or clutter. It reads politely, following each site's rules.

TAKES
The site list from Discovery
GIVES
Thousands of pages as clean text, plus YouTube titles, views and transcripts

Reads a competitor's new blog post and a Short with 2 million views on the same topic.

AGENT 04 · LOCAL EMBEDDINGS

Filter

The gatekeeper
NIGHT

Checks each page: is this about our world? It compares the page's meaning with your KB. Related pages pass; unrelated ones are thrown away, and their links are not followed, so the crawl doesn't wander off.

TAKES
Every page the Crawler read
GIVES
Only the relevant pages, roughly 1 in 10

A page on modular kitchens: kept. A page on cricket scores: dropped.

AGENT 05 · COGNEE

Knowledge

The librarian
NIGHT

Pulls the useful facts out of the relevant pages and files them in the KB. It skips what's already there, notes how sure it is, and retires old facts. Anything doubtful goes to a holding area, where no agent can use it until the manager approves it.

TAKES
Relevant pages from the Filter
GIVES
Safe learnings in the KB, doubtful ones in the holding area

Learns "before-and-after kitchen reels are trending this month". That's safe, so it's saved. "Competitor cut prices 30%" is doubtful, so it waits for the 6 pm review.

AGENT 06 · GPT RESEARCHER

Adapt

The translator
PRESS CRAWL

TAKES
Fresh trends from the KB, your company knowledge
GIVES
20–40 candidate ideas shaped to Magppie

Trend: "5 kitchen mistakes" reels. Adapted: "5 kitchen mistakes Magppie's design team fixes every week".

Strategist

TAKES
The 20–40 ideas from Adapt, your idea history
GIVES
6 ideas on your dashboard

Press Crawl again and it picks a different 6, skipping the ones you've already seen.

In simple words

Oracle server WHERE IT RUNS

A computer in Oracle's Mumbai data centre that never switches off. It is live now, paid for by Oracle's free trial credit for the first month. MAI and all 11 agents live there, so the crawl runs at night while your Mac is off.

Approval review MANAGER · 6 PM

Doubtful learnings, like a competitor's price or a fact that contradicts your KB, sit in a holding area and are never used until approved. Every day at 6 pm the manager gets a Chrome notification and a dashboard alert, then approves, rejects, edits or adds knowledge and presses Finalise review. Rejected items are remembered so they're never asked again. Obvious, safe learnings are saved automatically.

Local embeddings MEANING FINGERPRINT

A small free AI that turns each page into a "meaning fingerprint" and compares it with your KB's. Close match: keep. Far off: drop. "Local" means it runs on the Oracle server itself, so it's free and unlimited.

APIs and Apify HOW IT READS SOCIAL MEDIA

An API is an official way for software to read a platform's data within its rules. YouTube and Pinterest are read free with no key. Apify reads public Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn posts for us on its free monthly credit, checked once a night for new posts.

Cognee THE LEARNING ENGINE

Free software behind the Knowledge agent. It pulls facts out of pages, links them to what's already in your KB (products, competitors, trends), removes duplicates and retires old facts. It's what makes the KB smarter each night instead of just piling up pages.

SearXNG OUR OWN SEARCH ENGINE

A free search engine we run on the Oracle server. It searches Google, Bing and others for us, with no fees or limits. Discovery uses it to find new sites.

Crawl4AI THE PAGE READER

Free software that opens a web page like a browser does and turns it into clean text an AI can read.

Hermes Agent MAI'S BRAIN

Free agent software from Nous Research. It handles voice, gives out jobs, runs the schedule and keeps its own memory of how you work.

GPT Researcher RESEARCH + WRITING

Free software that reads your KB and web sources together and writes long, sourced text. It powers the Adapt agent and the Blog writer.

Database POSTGRES + PGVECTOR

A free database running on the server, the same engine Supabase is built on. It stores the knowledge base plus every idea, blog, creative and video.

Higgsfield MCP CONNECTOR

A connector that lets our server ask your Higgsfield account to make images and videos. You log in once; after that it works on its own.

RAG HOW IT THINKS

Before writing, the AI looks up the relevant parts of your KB and writes only from those, so every idea stays true to Magppie.

Layered AI brains GROQ · GEMINI · OPENROUTER

Three free AI services used together. Quick jobs go to Groq first, careful writing goes to Gemini first, and OpenRouter is the overflow. If one hits its free limit, MAI switches to the next automatically.

A day in the system

ONCE · SETUP

Load and connect

Upload the 11,000-line KB, the 90 script ideas, the crawler rules and the seed sites. Log in to Higgsfield once so the server can use your credits.

8 PM

MAI starts the night cycle

Discovery, Crawler, Filter and Knowledge run in a loop: find sites, read pages, keep the relevant ones, learn into the KB.

8 PM → 8 AM

Crawl and learn

Roughly 8k–30k pages visited, 800–2.5k kept, 300–800 new learnings, 30–150 new sites found. These are estimates until the first real runs.

8 AM

Overnight report

The crawler stops. The dashboard shows pages read, new sites and what the KB learned. Doubtful items wait in the holding area.

PRESS CRAWL · ~3–5 MIN

Get 6 ideas

Adapt and Strategist return 2 blog, 2 creative and 2 script ideas shaped to Magppie. Press again for a fresh set; earlier ideas never repeat.

PRESS FINALISE · ~15–35 MIN

Content gets made

Blogs take 3–5 minutes. Creatives and videos go through Higgsfield and take 10–30 minutes. Everything lands on the dashboard to watch, approve, download or regenerate.

6 PM · MANAGER

Daily approval review

The manager gets a Chrome notification and a dashboard alert, then approves, rejects or edits each doubtful item, adds any new knowledge, and presses Finalise review. Approved items are in the KB before the 8 pm crawl starts.

PHASE 2

Learn from results

Ad, analytics and CRM results flow back into the KB so future ideas lean toward what performs.
