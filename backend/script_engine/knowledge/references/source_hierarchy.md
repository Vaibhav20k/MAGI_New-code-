# Source Hierarchy & Validation Standards

## Tier 1: Primary Academic, Institutional & Official Standards
- Peer-reviewed research journals (Elsevier, Springer Nature, Wiley, IEEE, Taylor & Francis)
- Archaeological reports (ASI, UNESCO World Heritage Documentation, British Museum Annals)
- Architectural standards & building codes (LEED, WELL Building Standard, IGBC, NBC)
- Material standards (ASTM, ISO, DIN, BIS)

## Tier 2: Authoritative Monographs & Technical Handbooks
- University press architectural and historical books (Oxford, Cambridge, MIT Press)
- Materials science engineering handbooks
- Certified laboratory test reports

## Tier 3: Reputable Industry & Science Journalism
- Architectural Digest, Domus, Detail Magazine, Scientific American, New Scientist
- Major established news outlets with factual verification desks

## Tier 4: General Online References
- Manufacturer whitepapers (must be cross-verified for commercial bias)
- General architectural magazines and blogs

## Tier 5: Prohibited as Sole Authority
- Unsourced social media (Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn posts without primary links)
- Unmoderated wiki sites and personal opinion blogs
- Unverified folklore, viral reels, and ungrounded marketing copy
